Simple connections

How to play.

Sixteen words hide four groups of four. Work out what links each group, then find all four.

The basics.

  1. Pick fourSelect the four words you think belong together, then press Submit.
  2. Right or wrongA right guess locks the group in and shows its name. A wrong one costs one of your four mistakes. "One away" means three of your four belong together.
  3. Stuck?Press Hint to see the name of a group you haven't found. It costs a point.

Watch out for traps. Some words seem to fit two groups. "Snow" could make a snowball or a snowman. Only one way of splitting the sixteen words leaves every group complete.

Scoring.

Every puzzle starts worth 4 points. Each mistake and each hint knocks 1 off.

Points for one puzzle
Solved with no mistakes4 points
Solved with 1 mistake3 points
Solved with 2 mistakes2 points
Solved with 3 mistakes1 point
Out of guesses (4 mistakes)0 points
Each hint taken−1 pointA puzzle never scores below 0.
Time runs out mid-puzzle1 point per group foundUp to what the puzzle is still worth.

For example. Solve a puzzle with 1 mistake and 1 hint: 4 − 1 − 1 = 2 points. Time runs out when you've found 2 groups with no mistakes: 2 points. Time runs out at 2 groups and 3 mistakes: 1 point, because that's all the puzzle was still worth.

  • Your score is the total across all the puzzles.
  • Ties go to the team that finished all its puzzles quicker, timed from its first move.
  • Teams still playing at the end are ranked by groups found, then by who found them first.
