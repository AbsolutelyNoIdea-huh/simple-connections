Values Month, week 4: Simple solutions

Simple connections.

Sixteen words hide four groups of four. Spot what links them, then find the group. Three puzzles, each with its own theme.

  1. Find a groupSelect the four words you think belong together, then press Submit.
  2. Four mistakesEach puzzle allows four wrong guesses. Watch out for words that seem to fit more than one group. Stuck? A hint names one group, for 1 point.
  3. Fewer mistakes, more pointsA clean sweep is 4 points, then 3, 2, 1. If time runs out mid-puzzle, each group you found still scores. Twelve points are on the table.
